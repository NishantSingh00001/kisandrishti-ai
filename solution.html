The Solution

Instant Edge-AI
Crop Doctor

An offline-first Android app that diagnoses crop disease from a leaf photo and delivers voice treatment guides in your language — in under 1.5 seconds.

Core Capabilities

Three pillars of KisanDrishti

📷

Zero-Internet Leaf Scan

On-device TensorFlow Lite vision model classifies 40+ crop diseases from a single leaf photo. No internet required — ever, for the core diagnosis.

🎙️

Voice-First Regional Guidance

Bhashini ASR/TTS APIs deliver step-by-step treatment instructions as audio in Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, and Telugu. Farmers hear, not read.

🌿

Precision Dosage Alerts

Organic and low-cost chemical treatment steps are tailored per disease and crop type, with dosage amounts and application timing.

How It Works

Four-step field diagnosis

1

Capture Leaf

Snap a photo with any budget Android camera. No special hardware or lighting needed.

2

Edge Inference

TFLite model classifies the disease on-device in under 1.5 seconds.

3

Voice Advisory

Plays step-by-step cure in local dialect with dosage and timing.

4

Outbreak Sync

Auto-syncs regional disease data to cloud when connectivity returns.
