The Problem

Delayed Pest & Disease
Detection

India loses up to 35% of crop yield every year because farmers can't identify diseases fast enough — or at all.

⚠️

Severe Annual Crop Loss

Late pathogen identification is the leading cause of preventable yield loss in Indian agriculture. By the time a farmer gets an expert opinion, the disease has already spread.

35% average yield lost to late diagnosis
📶

Rural Connectivity Gaps

Existing agritech solutions are cloud-dependent. In low-signal farm zones — which cover most of rural India — these apps are simply unavailable when farmers need them most.

600M+ Indians in low-connectivity regions
🔤

Language & Literacy Barriers

English-only or text-heavy interfaces exclude the vast majority of smallholder farmers. Voice-first, vernacular interfaces are essential for real adoption at scale.

22+ official Indian languages, most underserved
Rural smallholder farmer, India

Why It Matters

The tools exist.
The access doesn't.

Modern AI can identify crop disease from a photo in milliseconds. But every existing solution requires a smartphone with internet, an English-literate user, and a stable connection — three things most Indian farmers don't have simultaneously.

120M+ smallholder farmers cultivate 86% of India's agricultural holdings — yet they remain the least served by agritech.
The average smallholder farm is under 2 hectares. A single disease outbreak can wipe out an entire season's income.
